Unisport Reservation System is an online tool used to enhance the process of booking university sports facilities that were designed by us for the university facility reservation system at University Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). The main purpose of this facility is to enhance the process of accessing the sports facilities among the students and staff at the university. Our facility is consistent with SDG 3 where our understanding is that the ease of access to physical activities among students is essential for improving their health and well-being.

At present, the sports facilities in UTeM range from gymnasiums to playing grounds for the students and faculty members. Currently, the facility booking process at UTeM is disorganized, and no systematic approach is used to facilitate reservations, which may cause problems for the user as he or she may end up missing out on some valuable timeslots due to lack of clarity regarding the availability of certain sports facilities. Most students and staff at UTeM tend not to book any of these facilities due to lack of knowledge about the booking procedure.

Unisport Reservation System is aware of the challenges and offers a web interface which makes the whole process of booking easier from start to finish. It allows users to see the current status of a particular sports facility at any time on the specified date, making it easy for them to make their choice and complete the booking process. The platform also offers features such as creating an account, logging in, and cancellation of reservations.
